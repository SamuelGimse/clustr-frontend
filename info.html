About Clustr Market

Clustr Market connects world events, source news and listed companies. It groups related stories into market themes so you can explore how a stock connects to the news around it.

The app is operated by Gimse Media. AI helps filter, assess and group news. Source links and report timestamps let you check the context yourself.

Clustr Market is a research and information app. It does not execute trades, manage investments or guarantee returns. A news impact score is not a stock price target or a recommendation to buy or sell.

Plans

Free accounts can browse older reports. Reports dated today or yesterday in US Eastern time, personal Watchlist and History require Pro. Pro also unlocks the Stocks and Trends views. Account shows your actual access and any billing options.

Lifetime Pro is a complimentary Pro grant with no expiry date or recurring payment. Places in the introductory offer must be claimed; signing up alone does not reserve one.

How to use the app

Start with a report and a scope

  1. Choose a report using the report controls or Reports. Its date determines the news context you see.
  2. Use All stocks / My watchlist to control the scope. In watchlist mode, the Links filter chooses which stock relationships count.
  3. Open a News card to see its articles, linked stocks and history. You can click the upper part of the card to open or close it.

Impact and price are different

Impact runs from −10 to +10. Positive scores describe constructive news; negative scores describe cautious news. Larger magnitudes give stronger article ratings more weight when the cluster score is combined. An impact of +5 does not mean a stock will rise 5%.

Price changes are shown separately. Read the label: a session change, change since a report, and a historical interval return measure different periods. A saved price belongs to its report snapshot; a latest quote has its own timestamp and can be delayed or unavailable.

Stock connections

Mentioned in articles shows recorded article mentions. Mentioned in clusters also includes close links, related stocks and theme comparison baskets. A basket relationship does not mean the source article names the company.

In Stocks, search by ticker or company and select a tile. Desktop shows the stock list on the left and linked clusters on the right. On mobile, select a stock to open its details and use Back to stocks to return.

Overview and Network

Selecting a stock in the Overview watchlist updates its news and network preview. Opening that preview in Network keeps the same stock, report and time window.

Network combines connections over the selected 24-, 48- or 72-hour window. Use the cluster strip above the graph or search your watchlist below it. Select a node to inspect its connected clusters, impacts and available saved prices.

Watchlist, movers and historical charts

Add or remove stocks using the star controls or Watchlist. My watchlist also filters Session movers. If a panel is empty, check your scope, enabled link types, selected report and search before assuming its data is missing.

History separates news impact from price performance. Trend fits use historical observations; the best R² describes fit to that sample. A projected line and its uncertainty band are model estimates, not a verified prediction of tomorrow’s price.

Notifications and appearance

Account contains appearance settings and an optional publish-notification switch. Notifications require browser permission and can be turned off again. On iPhone or iPad, push notifications require opening the installed Home Screen app.

Technology & credits

The current publish pipeline uses DeepSeek for AI analysis, through the OpenAI JavaScript SDK configured for DeepSeek’s API. The SDK credit does not mean current reports use an OpenAI model.

JavaScript powers the interactive app. Supabase provides account and data services, GitHub Pages hosts the frontend, and Stripe handles subscription checkout where enabled. News, quote and attention sources are identified alongside their data; their availability and coverage can vary.

Services and outside links

Supabase processes account and saved app data. Stripe processes checkout where used. An optional social sign-in provider processes that sign-in. Hosting, font and library delivery services receive requests when their resources load. Following a news link opens that publisher’s service under its own policies.

The current AI publish pipeline sends news and market context to DeepSeek for analysis. Its configured OpenAI SDK client sends those requests to DeepSeek’s endpoint. The publish prompts do not include your account password or payment-card details.

Browser storage and your controls

Signing out removes the stored app session. You can clear local preferences through your browser’s site-data controls; clearing browser storage does not itself delete server-side account or watchlist records. You can remove saved stocks in Watchlist and disable notifications in Account or browser settings.
