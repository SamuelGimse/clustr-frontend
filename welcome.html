02 / WHAT IS A CLUSTER?

A theme, not
a pile of headlines.

A cluster brings related stories together under a shared market theme. AI infrastructure, energy supply and cybersecurity are examples. One cluster can contain several separate events—and those events can point in different directions.

Clustr Market uses AI to help filter, group and assess news. You can then follow the original sources and explore the company connections yourself.

05 / BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little more context.

Does a positive score mean I should buy?

No. Scores describe an AI-assessed directional news impact, from −10 to +10. They are not probabilities, expected percentage returns, price targets or personal investment advice. News can already be priced in, and the assessment can be wrong.

Are the news and prices live?

Reports are published snapshots, not a continuous real-time news stream. Stock quotes show the latest available regular-session data and may be delayed. Session change and change since a report are different measures; check their labels and timestamps.

What do the different stock links mean?

“Mentioned” means the company is named in the article. Close and related links are research associations. A theme comparison basket provides sector context; it is not evidence that the company is directly affected.

Can I place trades here?

No. Clustr Market is a research and information app. It does not execute trades, manage your portfolio or guarantee investment results.

Do I need to pay to create an account?

No. Create a Free account with Google, Facebook or email. Pro is optional and costs 99 kr per month. Your Account page shows your actual access and subscription options.
